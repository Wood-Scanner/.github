# WoodScanner
## Every Piece of Wood Has Value

### 3-Minute Pitch

---

## 1. The Problem (0:00 - 0:30)

Every day, woodworking companies generate large amounts of leftover material.

- Valuable residual wood is often sold far below its actual value or treated as waste.
- Companies lack visibility into what inventory they really have.
- Buyers struggle to discover available materials and suppliers.

### Result

Valuable resources remain unused while buyers continue paying higher prices for raw materials.

---

## 2. Why Existing Solutions Fail (0:30 - 1:00)

Today, the market is highly fragmented.

- Buying and selling often relies on personal connections.
- Inventory management is largely manual.
- Residual wood dimensions and quality are rarely measured objectively.
- No centralized platform exists to connect supply and demand.

### The Core Issue

Sellers do not know exactly what they have.

Buyers do not know exactly what is available.

---

## 3. Our Solution (1:00 - 1:30)

WoodScanner automatically converts wood residue into a digital inventory.

### Using Computer Vision

- Capture an image of the wood piece
- Measure dimensions automatically
- Classify the material
- Upload inventory data to a marketplace

### Measured Attributes

- Length
- Width
- Thickness
- Surface area
- Volume

Instead of becoming waste, leftover wood becomes a sellable product.

---

## 4. How It Works (1:30 - 2:00)

### Step 1
Residual wood reaches the collection area.

### Step 2
WoodScanner captures an image.

### Step 3
AI analyzes and measures the piece.

### Step 4
Inventory is automatically generated.

### Step 5
Buyers can discover available stock in real time.

### Step 6
Transportation and sale can be arranged through the platform.

### Outcome

Wood waste becomes digital inventory in seconds.

---

The woodworking industry generates large volumes of reusable residual materials.

WoodScanner creates:

- A digital marketplace connecting buyers and sellers
- A growing database of supply and demand

Transform wood residue from a disposal problem into a valuable resource.
